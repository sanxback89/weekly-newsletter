Shein & Temu Face 54% Tariffs After De Minimis Closure; Pivot to Local Fulfillment

Summary: Following the closure of the de minimis loophole and imposition of tariffs up to 54% on Chinese low-value packages, Shein and Temu have dramatically restructured operations. Details:

  • De minimis loophole closed May 2025; initial 120% tariff/$100 fee imposed
  • Temporarily reduced to 54% under U.S.-China trade agreement (90-day negotiation)
  • Temu: U.S. sales now managed exclusively by local sellers with domestic fulfillment
  • Shein: Tariffs included in listed prices (no surprise delivery charges)
  • Consumer impact: $10 T-shirt could cost $22; $200 luggage set → $300
  • EU also imposed €3 flat surcharge on all packages under €150
