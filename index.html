  • Amazon: $716.9B annual revenue vs. Walmart: $713.2B — first time Amazon leads in total revenue
  • Amazon 연간 매출 $716.9B vs. Walmart $713.2B — Amazon이 처음으로 앞서
  • Comparison nuanced: Amazon includes AWS cloud and tech services; Walmart is predominantly physical retail
  • 단순 비교 한계: Amazon은 AWS 클라우드·기술 서비스 포함; Walmart는 주로 오프라인 소매

  • South Africa: Shein+Temu combined 3.6% clothing market share vs. H&M+Zara+Cotton On's 3.4% — achieved in just 5 years vs. 13 years for traditional brands
