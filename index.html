Gap Inc. Creates Chief Entertainment Officer Role, Names Former Paramount Exec Pam Kaufman

Summary: Gap Inc. has created the brand-new position of Chief Entertainment Officer (CXO) and appointed former Paramount executive Pam Kaufman to lead its entertainment, content, and licensing strategy. The move signals a major strategic pivot toward what CEO Richard Dickson calls "fashiontainment."

Headline:

  • Pam Kaufman previously served as President and CEO of International Markets, Global Consumer Products and Experiences at Paramount.
  • The new CXO role is designed to build and scale Gap Inc.'s presence across music, television, film, sports, gaming, consumer products, and cultural collaborations.
  • CEO Richard Dickson stated: "Fashion is entertainment, and today's customers aren't just buying apparel — they're buying into brands that tell compelling stories and drive cultural conversations."
  • The announcement follows Gap Inc.'s "Better in Denim" campaign featuring KATSEYE and Harlem's Fashion Row, reinforcing its focus on cultural relevance.
  • Kaufman reports directly to President and CEO Richard Dickson and started February 2, 2026.

주요 내용:

  • 팸 카우프만은 파라마운트에서 글로벌 소비자 제품 및 국제 시장 총괄 사장을 역임했습니다.
  • 신설 CXO 역할은 음악·TV·영화·스포츠·게임·문화 협업 전반에 걸친 갭의 존재감을 확장하는 것을 목표로 합니다.

Walmart Q4 FY26: Revenue $190.7B (+5.6%), E-commerce Up 27% — 15th Consecutive Quarter of Double-Digit Growth

Summary: Walmart reported strong Q4 FY26 results on February 19, 2026, with revenue rising 5.6% to $190.7 billion and e-commerce surging 27% YoY — marking the 15th straight quarter of double-digit digital gains. E-commerce now accounts for a record 23% of U.S. sales.

Headline:

  • Adjusted EPS of $0.74 beat consensus estimate of $0.73; operating income grew 10.8%, outpacing sales.
  • Comparable store sales rose 4.6% in the U.S. and 4.0% at Sam's Club (ex-fuel).
  • Store-fulfilled delivery grew approximately 50%; Walmart Connect (advertising) sales rose ~41%.
  • Net income declined to $4.24 billion ($0.53/share) from $5.25 billion ($0.65/share) year-ago due to one-time items.
  • Walmart authorized a new $30 billion share repurchase program, replacing a previous $20 billion authorization.
  • 53rd consecutive annual dividend increase: payout raised 5% to $0.99/share.

Walmart Issues Cautious FY27 Guidance
Retailer – Walmart HIGH February 19, 2026 Bloomberg

Walmart Issues Cautious FY27 Guidance, Shares Drop ~3%; New CEO John Furner Signals Flexibility

Summary: Despite strong Q4 results, Walmart's conservative FY27 guidance disappointed Wall Street. The retailer forecasts adjusted EPS of $2.75–$2.85, well below analyst expectations of $2.97. New CEO John Furner, who officially took the helm February 1, cited trade uncertainty and a "K-shaped" consumer recovery.

Headline:

  • FY27 adjusted EPS guidance: $2.75–$2.85 vs. analyst consensus of $2.97.
  • Shares fell approximately 3% on the cautious outlook.
  • CEO John Furner noted persistent K-shaped consumer dynamics: higher-income households (earning $100K+) are increasingly choosing Walmart, while lower-income consumers face cost-of-living pressure.
  • For the first time in history, Amazon ($716.9B revenue for 2025) surpassed Walmart ($713.2B) as the world's largest company by annual revenue.
  • Management cited the need for flexibility amid unpredictable trade conditions and tariff risks.

요약: 강한 4분기 실적에도 불구하고 월마트의 보수적인 FY27 가이던스가 시장을 실망시켰습니다. 조정 EPS 전망치 $2.75~$2.85는 애널리스트 컨센서스 $2.97을 크게 하회했으며, 신임 CEO 존 퍼너는 무역 불확실성과 "K자형" 소비 양극화를 주요 요인으로 꼽았습니다.

주요 내용:

  • FY27 조정 EPS 가이던스: $2.75~$2.85 (시장 컨센서스 $2.97 대비 하회).
  • 주가 발표 직후 약 3% 하락.
  • 아마존이 2025년 연매출 $7,169억으로 월마트($7,132억)를 최초로 앞지르며 세계 최대 기업 등극.

Kohl's Q3 Net Sales Decline Nearly 3%; CEO Michael Bender Deepens Turnaround Strategy

Summary: Kohl's continues to face headwinds as Q3 2025 net sales declined nearly 3% YoY.
