Trade CRITICAL February 2026 Business of Fashion

US Apparel Tariff Rates Spike to 23.8%; China at Unprecedented 69.1%

Summary: The weighted average tariff on US apparel and footwear imports spiked from 13% to 36% (peaked at 54%). China-origin apparel faces 69.1% tariffs. Over 80% of fashion companies plan to further reduce China sourcing by 2027.

Details:

  • Average US apparel tariff: 23.8% (May 2025), up from 13.9% a year prior
  • China tariff: 69.1% (May 2025), up from 22.1% in January 2025
  • Vietnam tariff negotiated down from 46% to 20%
  • Bangladesh tariff: reduced from 37% → 35% → 20% → now 19% under new trade deal
  • Key sourcing hubs (China, Vietnam, India, Bangladesh, Indonesia) = 63% of US imports, ~75% of tariff impact
  • Supreme Court reviewing tariff legality; experts expect possible rollback

Trade CRITICAL February 2026 SupplyChainBrain

De Minimis Exemption Abolished: US Parcels Drop 54%; EU Imposes €3 Duty from July 2026

Summary: The US eliminated the $800 de minimis exemption that fueled Shein and Temu's growth. Parcels under $800 to the US dropped 54%. The EU will impose a €3 customs fee on sub-€150 parcels starting July 2026.

Details:

  • US: de minimis eliminated for China/Hong Kong in May 2025, later extended globally
  • Sub-$800 parcels to the US fell 54% (Universal Postal Union data)
  • EU: €3 flat customs duty on parcels < €150 from July 1, 2026 — two years ahead of schedule
  • France lobbying for €5 per parcel; citing inaccurate value declarations by Chinese exporters
  • Shein and Temu adapted: shifted to US/EU warehouse-based distribution models

  • France eco-score: Full enforcement October 2026; non-compliance fines up to 5% of annual revenue
  • EU Empowering Consumers Directive: National implementation by March 2026, effective September 2026
  • California SB 253: Scope 1 & 2 emissions reporting starts 2026 for companies >$1B revenue
  • California SB 707: Textile producers must participate in approved PRO by July 1, 2026
