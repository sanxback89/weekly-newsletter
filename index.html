Summary: Walmart is testing a rapid four-week remodeling process for its Neighborhood Market stores across six states to reduce customer disruption and improve store layouts. Details:세부사항:

  • 10 test locations across Florida, Oklahoma, Texas, Georgia, South Carolina, and Louisiana
  • Four-week remodel cycle versus traditional months-long process, with main sales floor closed but pharmacies and fuel stations remaining open
  • Improvements include expanded aisles, reconfigured layouts, upgraded online grocery pickup and delivery areas, digital price signage
